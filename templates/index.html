📚 Risk Scoring Methodology

The Risk-Aware DevSecOps Security Gate calculates a numerical risk score from security findings detected during Pull Request analysis.

1️⃣ Severity Weights

Severity Risk Points
BLOCKER / CRITICAL 10
HIGH 7
MEDIUM / MODERATE / MAJOR 4
LOW / MINOR 2
Unknown / Unclassified 0

2️⃣ Finding Impact

  • Secret findings: use their reported severity.
  • IAM wildcard findings: are treated as HIGH severity and contribute 7 base risk points.
  • Dependency vulnerabilities: use their reported vulnerability severity.
  • SonarCloud findings: use their reported severity.

3️⃣ Diminishing Impact

  • 1st finding: 100%
  • 2nd finding: 75%
  • 3rd finding: 50%
  • 4th finding: 35%
  • 5th and later: 20%

The diminishing multiplier is applied separately within each scanner category.

4️⃣ SonarCloud Quality Gate

A failed SonarCloud Quality Gate adds 5 additional risk points.

5️⃣ Final Score

Final Risk Score = Sum of all finding contributions + Quality Gate penalty

The resulting value is rounded to the nearest whole number and capped at a maximum of 100.

6️⃣ Risk Levels

Score Risk Level
0 – 20 LOW
21 – 40 MEDIUM
41 – 70 HIGH
71 – 100 CRITICAL

7️⃣ Risk Score vs Final Verdict

The numerical risk score does not independently determine whether a Pull Request is allowed.

The final verdict is determined by mandatory security blocking conditions.

  • Any detected secret → BLOCK
  • Any IAM wildcard → BLOCK
  • Critical, High, Medium or Moderate dependency vulnerability → BLOCK
  • SonarCloud execution failure → BLOCK
  • Failed SonarCloud Quality Gate → BLOCK
  • Any SonarCloud finding → BLOCK
  • No mandatory blocking condition → ALLOW

📝 Important Design Principle

Risk Score = quantitative risk measurement
Final Verdict = security policy decision
